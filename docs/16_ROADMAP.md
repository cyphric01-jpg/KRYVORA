# 16. Roadmap

## 16.1 Planning Principle

KRYVORA's roadmap is guided by evidence integrity, safer destructive workflow enforcement, operational clarity, and measured extensibility. The roadmap focuses on improving a strong working platform rather than expanding into unsupported or over-broad claims.

## 16.2 Phased Roadmap

| Phase | Theme | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core evidence integrity | High |
| 2 | Recovery coverage expansion | High |
| 3 | Policy enforcement for sanitization | High |
| 4 | Provenance and job linkage completion | High |
| 5 | Reporting and audit refinement | Medium |
| 6 | Validation and benchmark expansion | Medium |
| 7 | Hardening and security review | Medium |
| 8 | Enterprise-grade extension | Future |

## 16.3 Near-Term Objectives

1. strengthen policy integration for file and folder workflow enforcement
2. improve report verification and confidence reconstruction
3. expand execution coverage for supported evidence types and candidate states
4. extend audit provenance completeness for jobs and report relationships
5. document harder validation and broader benchmark coverage

## 16.4 Release Gates

Future maturity milestones should require:

- clear evidence integrity proof for each workflow
- test coverage for success and failure paths
- credible audit and provenance records
- transparent documentation of boundaries and limits
- independent review before expanding destructive or high-risk operations
